# CairnKV v0.3.0 — ReadIndex & Partition Safety

Reads are now explicit: linearizable by default via ReadIndex, stale by opt-in.
Fault injection proves that an isolated leader cannot serve a linearizable read.

## Highlights

- `Get` defaults to ReadIndex: the leader waits for its current-term no-op to commit, records `readIndex`, confirms leadership with a heartbeat majority, then serves after `lastApplied >= readIndex`.
- `kStale` reads are explicit local reads and return `applied_index`.
- Pending reads queue until the leader is ready and fail with a leader hint on step-down.
- A test/ops partition hook (`cairnctl partition <ids>` / `unpartition`) drops Raft traffic in both directions.
- `scripts/partition-smoke.sh` validates minority behavior, majority progress, and rejoin convergence.

## Correctness

- ReadIndex is served only after a current-term entry is committed.
- An isolated old leader cannot obtain a majority heartbeat round, so it cannot serve a linearizable read.
- Stale reads are local and may be old; this is documented in the API.
- The majority partition elects a new leader and keeps serving; the minority cannot commit.
- After rejoin, the old leader steps down and its log/apply indexes converge with the new leader.

## Validation

- Build: 51/51.
- `ctest`: 7/7.
- Cluster smoke: election, kill leader, re-election, committed data retained, follower redirect.
- Partition smoke:
  - isolated leader stale read returned the local applied value;
  - isolated leader linearizable read timed out (`rc=124`);
  - a new leader was elected in a higher term and continued serving;
  - after unpartition, all replicas converged to the same commit/apply index.

## Known limits

- Single Raft group; no sharding or multi-Raft.
- `Scan` remains leader-only; ReadIndex covers single-key `Get` in this release.
- No snapshot, lease read, or dynamic membership.
