# CairnKV v0.2.0 — Raft Core

A single-group Raft KV milestone: leader election, replicated logs, commit, and leader redirect.

## Highlights

- Leader election with randomized timeouts and the log up-to-date vote check.
- AppendEntries with `prevLogIndex/prevLogTerm`, conflict suffix truncation, and fsync before success.
- Current-term commit rule; a leader appends a no-op to safely advance `commitIndex`.
- Per-peer transport and a single Raft core thread; client requests are offloaded from I/O threads.
- Follower → leader redirect with automatic client reconnect and retry.

## Validation

- Build: 51/51.
- Tests: 7/7 (`ctest`).
- Multi-process cluster: election, put/get, kill leader, re-election, committed data retained.
- Put/get through a follower redirects to the leader.

## Known limits

- Single Raft group; no sharding or multi-Raft.
- Leader-only reads; ReadIndex arrives in v0.3.0.
- No snapshot, lease read, or dynamic membership.
